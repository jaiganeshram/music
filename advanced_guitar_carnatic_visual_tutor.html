🌊 Carnatic Gamaka Physics on Acoustic Guitar

How to execute authentic Carnatic Gamakas on steel guitar strings:

1. Kampitha (Microtonal Oscillation / Bend)

Gentle sideways deflection of the guitar string back-and-forth across the fret wire. Crucial for Gandharam in Kalyani, Sankarabharanam, and Nishadam in Todi.

2. Jaaru (Meend / Glissando Slide)

Smooth slide from one fret to another without releasing finger pressure. Ullaasu Jaaru (Upward Slide) & Dhallu Jaaru (Downward Slide).

3. Nokku (Downward Hammer Strike)

Plucking a note and sharply pressing down from a higher grace note (Anuswaram) with subtle microtonal rebound.

4. Orikkai (Flick / Pull-Off Grace)

Momentary snap of the left-hand finger releasing off the fret to touch the higher swara momentarily.

5. Odukkal (Gliding Deflection)

Gradual increase of finger tension pulling the string perpendicular to the fretboard to raise the pitch by 1 or 2 microtones.

6. Khandippu (Staccato Cut)

Sharp, crisp articulation stopping the string vibration abruptly before jumping to the next swara.
