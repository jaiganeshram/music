Day 19 · Slow down and hum it out

Your song-by-ear workbench

Choose a simple song you already know. This book does not provide a song list or recorded song tracks, so use a source you choose and work out its melody by listening.

1 · Slow the song down

Load an audio file from your device. It plays locally in this browser tab; it is not uploaded. The book recommends 50–70% speed and many listening/humming repetitions.

0.60×
Loop not set

Listen to the whole melody slowly, then repeat a short section until the sound is clear. Sing on “aa” or “oo” rather than focusing on words.

2 · Count your listening rounds

Before touching the keys, listen and hum. The guide suggests roughly 15–20 repeats before moving on.

3 · Break the phrase into syllables

Clap once for each small sung sound. Keep your own phrase labels; no lyrics are needed here.

Day 20–21 · Pitch match and relative pitch

Find the first note, then follow its neighbours

Hum only the first pitch, compare it with one key at a time, and mark a match. For each next note, ask: same, higher, lower, small move or larger jump?
