📖 Staff Notation Fundamentals for Guitar Hal Leonard & Mel Bay Concepts
🎼 1. The 5-Line Staff & Treble Clef
Music is written on 5 horizontal lines and 4 spaces between them. Guitar music uses the Treble Clef (𝄞), also called the G-Clef because its loop wraps around Line 2 (G4).

Lines from Bottom to Top: E4, G4, B4, D5, F5 (Mnemonic: Every Good Boy Does Fine).
Spaces from Bottom to Top: F4, A4, C5, E5 (Spells F-A-C-E).
⏱ 2. Note Values & Durations
Whole Note (𝅝): Hollow circle with no stem = 4 Beats (Count 1-2-3-4).

Half Note (𝅗𝅥): Hollow circle with vertical stem = 2 Beats (Count 1-2 or 3-4).

Quarter Note (𝅘𝅥): Solid filled circle with stem = 1 Beat (Count 1, 2, 3, 4).
🎸 3. Pick Strokes & Technique Signs
Downstroke Sign (⊓): Strike the guitar string with a downward motion of your plectrum/pick.

Finger Arching: Keep the left-hand fingers arched perpendicular to the fretboard so fingertips do not mute adjacent strings.
