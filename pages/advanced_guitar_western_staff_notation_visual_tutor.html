📖 Advanced Classical Guitar Techniques Conservatory Fundamentals
1. Right-Hand P-I-M-A Technique

p (pulgar): Thumb strikes down on bass strings 4, 5, 6.
i (índice): Index finger strikes upward on string 3.
m (medio): Middle finger strikes upward on string 2.
a (anular): Ring finger strikes upward on treble string 1.

2. Rest Stroke (Apoyando) vs Free Stroke (Tirando)

Apoyando (Rest Stroke): Finger strikes string and rests on the adjacent lower string for maximum volume and warmth.
Tirando (Free Stroke): Finger curves into palm without touching adjacent strings for fast arpeggios.

3. Fretboard Positions (Pos I to XII)

Position is defined by where the 1st finger rests. Pos V means 1st finger is on Fret 5, 2nd finger on Fret 6, 3rd finger on Fret 7, 4th finger on Fret 8.
